Mo Huang (Senior Member, IEEE) received the Ph.D. degrees from Sun Yat-sen University, Guangzhou, China, in 2014.

From 2008 to 2014, he was an IC Design Engineer and Project Manager with Rising Microelectronic Ltd., Guangzhou, China. From 2015 to 2016, he was a Post-Doctoral Fellow with University of Macau, China. From 2017 to 2019, he was with South China University of Technology, Guangzhou, China, as an Associate Professor. In 2019, he joined Institute of Microelectronics, University of Macau, China, where he is currently an Associate Professor. His current research interests are power management integrated circuits and systems. He has authored a book Revealing Hybrid DC-DC Converters (Springer, 2025).

Dr. Huang has served as Technical Program Committee member of ISSCC and CICC. He was a recipient of the ISSCC 2017 Takuo Sugano Award for Outstanding Far-East Paper, and the CICC 2025 Best Regular Paper.
